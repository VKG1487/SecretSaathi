Confidential & Student-Centric

A simple first step toward understanding how you're feeling.

SecretSaathi helps students reflect on common stress indicators through a short, non-diagnostic self-assessment and connects them with appropriate support resources.

Start Self-Assessment → Explore Resources

SecretSaathi is not a medical diagnostic service. It provides general awareness and support-resource information.

ⓘ
Important Notice: SecretSaathi does not provide medical diagnoses, psychological treatment, or crisis intervention. If you are experiencing acute distress or need immediate medical assistance, please contact national emergency services (112) or call the Tele-MANAS helpline (14416).
