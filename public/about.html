About SecretSaathi

SecretSaathi is a non-diagnostic student well-being self-assessment and resource navigation platform designed to bridge the gap between student hesitation and timely support.

In college and university environments, students frequently encounter multifaceted pressures—from demanding coursework deadlines, competition, and examination anxiety to interpersonal shifts, financial constraints, and feelings of isolation. While many institutions maintain student counselling and welfare offices, students often delay seeking help because they are unsure if their stress is "serious enough" or fear social stigma.

The Challenge

Students often navigate heavy emotional burdens alone due to several common barriers:

  • Hesitation to reach out: Difficulty articulating feelings or wondering whether daily pressure warrants formal support.
  • Fragmented information: Unawareness of available campus wellness cells, grievance mechanisms, or national 24/7 tele-health helplines.
  • Fear of judgment: Concerns regarding privacy, confidentiality, and institutional record-keeping.
  • Overwhelming clinical language: Being confronted with intimidating medical jargon rather than calm, approachable self-reflection tools.

Our Solution

SecretSaathi serves as an approachable, private, and confidential first step:

  • Anonymous Self-Reflection: A structured 10-question self-assessment covering academic pressure, rest patterns, focus, and social connection.
  • Non-Diagnostic Indicators: Transparent categorization (Low, Moderate, High) with grounded, practical lifestyle suggestions rather than medical labels.
  • Guided Rule-Based Chat: A focused conversational interface that helps students navigate recurring concerns without presenting itself as a clinical therapist.
  • Centralized Resource Directory: Searchable listings of both verified national crisis helplines and campus support contacts.

Technology Stack

SecretSaathi is deliberately built using clean, vanilla, and transparent technologies without heavy external frameworks:

  • Frontend: Semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (custom responsive design system), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
  • Backend: Node.js and Express.js REST API service.
  • Data Storage: Lightweight, editable JSON data structures (questions.json, resources.json, config.json).
  • Privacy Architecture: Client-side session state; zero database persistence of student scores or identities.
