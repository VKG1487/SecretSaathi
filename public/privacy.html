Medical & Psychological Disclaimer

ⓘ
CRITICAL NOTICE: SecretSaathi is NOT a medical, psychiatric, or psychological diagnostic tool. It is an educational and self-reflection web utility designed exclusively for general awareness.

The assessments, stress indicators, categories, and chatbot responses provided by SecretSaathi are intended solely for personal self-reflection. Under no circumstances should the contents of this website be considered:

  • A clinical diagnosis of depression, anxiety disorders, psychological distress, or mental illness.
  • A substitute for professional medical consultation, psychiatric evaluation, or clinical psychotherapy.
  • Emergency medical treatment or crisis intervention advice.

Never disregard professional medical or psychiatric advice, nor delay seeking professional guidance, because of information presented on this application. If you believe you may be experiencing a medical emergency or severe acute crisis, please immediately contact national emergency services (112 in India), visit the nearest hospital emergency room, or call the 24/7 toll-free Tele-MANAS helpline at 14416.

Privacy by Design & Data Minimization

SecretSaathi is engineered on a strict Data Minimization Principle. We believe that students should be able to explore their mental well-being without worrying about surveillance, advertising tracking, or data breaches.

1. No Account or Registration Required

You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal identifiers such as your name, student roll number, college email address, or phone number to access any part of SecretSaathi.

2. Zero Permanent Assessment Storage

Your answers during the self-assessment are evaluated in-memory during the session. Scores are temporarily kept in your browser's private sessionStorage so you can view your results. As soon as you close your browser tab or window, the stored assessment information is wiped from your device.

3. What You Should NOT Share

Users are advised never to input personally identifiable information (such as government IDs, credit card numbers, passwords, or full residential addresses) into the chatbot or search fields.

4. External Links & Resources

SecretSaathi provides links to third-party institutions, helplines, and government wellness portals. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, availability, or content of external websites. We encourage students to review the respective privacy statements of external resources when contacting them.

Maintenance of Support Resource Information

Helpline numbers, operating hours, and campus desk locations listed on SecretSaathi are curated from publicly available official directories and representative academic templates. Institutional coordinators can easily update and verify these records through the application's configuration and data files.

If you are a student welfare officer or campus coordinator wishing to update contact details, you can update the data/resources.json file on the server.
